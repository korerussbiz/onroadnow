How to buy (step by step)

  1. Install MetaMask or Trust Wallet, add the Polygon network.
  2. Buy a small amount of POL on an exchange (Binance works) and send it to your wallet.
  3. Click Connect Wallet above — this opens WalletConnect.
  4. Click Open QuickSwap, paste the ATHOL address into the "To" field.
  5. Review the price, slippage, and gas before confirming.

PayPal is not accepted for ATHOL. PayPal's policy prohibits cryptocurrency sales without pre-approval. Use a wallet.
